# ELSTER workflow and after-filing

## Before
1. Mein ELSTER account (Steuer-ID registration; the certificate is the user's, never the agent's).
2. Account → **Vorausgefüllte Steuererklärung / Belegabruf**: give consent once; data available ~1 day later; all of the last 4 years (E2).
3. Start the Einkommensteuererklärung for the year in Mein ELSTER and pick the Anlagen from the worksheet. Use "Datenübernahme aus einem Profil" for Steuernummer/Stammdaten if a profile exists (E1).
4. Enter Steuernummer (or choose "Neue Steuernummer beantragen" if none; Finanzamt = residence at filing).
5. Switch on the Antragsveranlagung checkbox on the start page if filing voluntarily (E1: "Startseite des Formulars").
6. Use ELSTER's Prüfen before Absenden; read all warnings, not only errors.

## Submit
User does it. Keep the PDF "Übertragungsprotokoll" and the document list. Receipts are not uploaded by default (Belegvorhaltepflicht): keep them and hand them over when the Finanzamt asks, at least until the Bescheid is final (longer while it carries a Vorbehalt der Nachprüfung).

## After (compare the Bescheid with the worksheet)
- Line-by-line: Brutto, Lohnsteuer, Werbungskosten (entered vs allowed), Sonderausgaben, Steuererstattung/-nachzahlung.
- **Einspruchsfrist: one month** from Bekanntgabe (§ 355 AO). Mail is deemed delivered on the 4th day after posting (§ 122 AO) [F3]. ELSTER offers an online Einspruch; name the specific line and the reason.
- "Vorbehalt der Nachprüfung" or "Vorläufigkeit" in the Bescheid keeps it changeable beyond the Einspruchsfrist, both ways [N6].
- Missed an item (e.g. a Spendenquittung found later)? Einspruch within the month; otherwise a change request is only possible while the Bescheid carries a Vorbehalt/Vorläufigkeit (N6) and, for voluntary returns, inside the 4-year window. Say which case applies; don't promise a correction.

## Do not
Automate the portal (terms of use; certificate exposure), send unsolicited receipts, or tell the user a number is certain before the Bescheid.
