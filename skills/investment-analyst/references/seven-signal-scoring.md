# 7-Signal Scoring Framework

Score any opportunity before investing. Each signal scored 0–3, then weighted.

## The Signals

| Signal | Weight | What to Look For |
|--------|--------|-----------------|
| Structural demand shift | 25% | New, irreversible demand the market hasn't quantified. Ask: "What does every player in [hot trend] NEED but nobody talks about?" |
| "Dead" narrative | 15% | Consensus wrote off sector as obsolete/dying. Screen for negative sentiment + declining analyst coverage + low P/E. |
| Earnings inflection | 20% | Revenue declining 2+ years → flat → first positive quarter. The flat-to-positive transition is the buy signal. |
| Supply constraint | 15% | Lead-time extensions, "fully allocated," price increases, inventory drawdowns. When supply is sold out 18 months forward = extreme pricing power. |
| Oligopoly position | 10% | 2-3 dominant players, customers cannot switch. Ask: "If I'm a customer, can I go anywhere else?" |
| Under-followed | 5% | < 10-15 analysts, especially after years of being "boring." No new coverage initiations in 2+ years = forgotten. |
| Technology catalyst | 10% | New product/tech unlocking step-change capability. R&D spending high for years and product finally shipping. |

## Scoring Guide

- **3** = Strong evidence, multiple confirming sources
- **2** = Moderate evidence, thesis plausible but not overwhelming
- **1** = Weak or mixed evidence
- **0** = No evidence or contradicting evidence

## Decision Thresholds

| Weighted Score | Conviction | Position Size |
|---------------|-----------|---------------|
| 2.5+ | High | Up to 5% of portfolio |
| 2.0–2.5 | Medium | 2–3% of portfolio |
| 1.5–2.0 | Low | Watchlist only |
| Below 1.5 | None | Pass |

## How to Calculate

```
Score = (Structural × 0.25) + (Dead × 0.15) + (Earnings × 0.20) + 
        (Supply × 0.15) + (Oligopoly × 0.10) + (Under-followed × 0.05) + 
        (Tech × 0.10)
```
