# Laggard Scanner — Reference Methodology

## Core Principle

When a Phase 3 company validates a theme with blowout earnings, the opportunity is NOT in buying that company at 40x EBITDA. The opportunity is in the Phase 2 company doing the same work at 15x that hasn't repriced yet.

**Validate with winners. Buy the laggards.**

---

## Why Valuation Gaps Exist

Same end-market, same demand driver, dramatically different multiples. Common reasons:

| Reason the Laggard is Cheap | Is It Fixable? | Example Pattern |
|-----------------------------|:--------------:|-----------------|
| Legacy execution issues | Yes (time + new backlog) | Old disputes roll off, new projects are better |
| Market misclassifies the business | Yes (segment growth changes narrative) | "Diversified industrial" → actually 50% grid |
| Recent IPO / thin coverage | Yes (analysts initiate, institutions discover) | 2-3 analysts → 8-10 over 18 months |
| Turnaround in progress | Maybe (execution dependent) | New management + secular tailwind |
| Structurally different business | No | Lower margins, lower barriers, commodity work |

The key question: **Is the valuation gap TEMPORARY (will close) or STRUCTURAL (will persist)?**

---

## The Filter Criteria

### Must-Have (all required)

1. **Same end-market as the Phase 3 winner** — serves same customers, same demand driver
2. **Backlog growing** — proves demand is reaching this company, not just the winners
3. **Fewer than 8 analysts** — institutional discovery still ahead
4. **PEG < 1.5 OR EV/EBITDA < 0.5x peer** — meaningful valuation gap exists
5. **RSI < 65** — not yet in momentum/chase phase
6. **Market cap $500M–$5B** — small enough to reprice 2-3x if narrative changes

### Nice-to-Have (increase conviction)

- Management explicitly mentions the theme (data centers, grid, AI, etc.)
- Recent contract win in the high-growth end market
- Insider buying or institutional accumulation (smart money entering)
- Acquisition by a Phase 3 peer would be logical (M&A optionality)
- Catalyst with defined timeline (earnings, certification, contract announcement)

### Red Flags (disqualify)

- Revenue/backlog shrinking despite theme strength → not capturing demand
- Analyst count INCREASING rapidly (5 → 12 in 6 months) → already being discovered
- RSI > 75 and 52w return > 200% → already repriced, you're late
- Short interest > 15% → something structural may be wrong
- No management commentary on the theme → they're not pivoting into it

## The Valuation Re-Rating Math

When a company transitions from "boring contractor" to "theme beneficiary," multiples expand:

```
Current: $500M revenue × 15x EV/EBITDA × 18% margin = $1.35B EV
Re-rated: $600M revenue × 25x EV/EBITDA × 20% margin = $3.0B EV

→ 2.2x upside from MULTIPLE EXPANSION + GROWTH + MARGIN IMPROVEMENT
```

The triple-driver (revenue growth + margin expansion + multiple expansion) is what creates 3-5x returns in 18-24 months for correctly identified laggards.

---

## Timing: When Does the Gap Close?

Typical repricing catalysts (in order of likelihood):

1. **Earnings beat with theme-specific commentary** (most common) — company reports and says "data center backlog now 30% of total"
2. **Analyst initiation** — bulge bracket firm initiates coverage, institutional buying follows
3. **Acquisition by Phase 3 peer** — validates the business quality at premium valuation
4. **ETF inclusion** — thematic ETF (GRID, VOLT) adds the stock, driving passive flows
5. **Conference presentation** — management presents at industry conference, visibility increases

Average timeline from Phase 2 to Phase 3 repricing: **12-24 months**

## Position Sizing

| Conviction | Thesis Clarity | Catalyst Timeline | Allocation |
|-----------|---------------|-------------------|-----------|
| High | Clear narrative mismatch, provable | < 6 months | 4-5% |
| Medium | Plausible but unproven | 6-12 months | 2-3% |
| Low | Speculative, needs more data | > 12 months or uncertain | 1-2% |

**Rule:** Never > 5% in a single name that hasn't proven public market execution. Phase 2 bets are portfolio diversifiers, not concentrated positions.

---

## Re-Running the Scan

**Frequency:** After each earnings season (quarterly)

**What changes between runs:**
- New Phase 3 winners emerge (add to validation set)
- Some laggards start reporting theme-specific growth (upgrade conviction)
- Some laggards get discovered (RSI/analyst count rises → may be too late)
- New IPOs or spinoffs enter the space (fresh candidates)

**Maintenance:** Remove candidates that:
- Cross RSI 75 and 200% 52w return (now Phase 3)
- Get covered by > 10 analysts (fully discovered)
- Report declining backlog/revenue (thesis broken)
- Get acquired (opportunity realized)
