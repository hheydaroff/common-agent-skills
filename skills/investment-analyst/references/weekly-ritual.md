# Weekly Research Ritual

30 minutes per week to build pattern recognition. The goal is detecting narrative-reality mismatches 6 months before the market.

## Monday — Constraint Language Scan (10 min)

**First: Portfolio Intelligence Quick Check (5 min)**

Before anything else, ask:
1. Did any holding report earnings since Friday? → Run signal propagation
2. Any holding move >10% in past week? → Why? Thesis strengthening or breaking?
3. Any catalyst in next 7 days for any holding? → Pre-catalyst prep
4. Current regime still favoring the same names? → Sizing still correct?

See [portfolio-intelligence.md](portfolio-intelligence.md) for full framework.

**Then: Pick ONE trade publication per week (rotate):**

| Publication | Focus | Region |
|-------------|-------|--------|
| Data Center Knowledge | Power, cooling, construction delays | US/Global |
| Power Magazine | Grid bottlenecks, transformer orders | US |
| ServeTheHome | Hardware shortages, networking | US/Global |
| Energy News Beat | Energy supply chain | US |
| Transformers Magazine | Transformer lead times & pricing | Global |
| Upstream Online / Rigzone | Nuclear, uranium, SMR progress | Global |
| Energy Monitor | European energy transition, grid | EU |
| Euractive | EU policy, regulation, industrial policy | EU |
| Janes Defence | European/global defense spending & procurement | EU/Global |
| Windpower Monthly | EU offshore wind, turbine supply | EU |
| Hydrogen Insight | EU hydrogen economy, electrolyzer supply | EU |
| S&P Global Commodity Insights | Metals, energy pricing | Global |
| ICIS | European chemical, energy commodity pricing | EU |
| Railway Gazette | European rail infrastructure & orders | EU |

**Scan for these literal phrases:**
- "Lead times extended to..." → supply constrained
- "Fully allocated through..." → sold out months/years ahead
- "Price increases of X%..." → pricing power confirmed
- "Customers competing for..." → demand > supply
- "Backlog reached record..." → future revenue locked in
- "Capacity expansion announced..." → demand confirmed real
- "Delayed due to lack of..." → bottleneck identified

**Output:** One line in journal noting what was found, who the players are.

## Wednesday — Earnings Surprise Check (10 min)

**Free screeners:**
- Zacks Earnings Surprise → filter by sector
- Yahoo Finance Earnings Calendar → sort by Surprise %
- MarketScreener → Sectors → Industrials/Utilities → Last EPS Surprise
- **EU:** MarketScreener.com → European stocks → filter by exchange (Xetra, Euronext, LSE)
- **EU:** justetf.com for sector ETF performance (STOXX 600 sectors)

**Filter for boring sectors:**
- Industrials (electrical equipment, machinery, construction)
- Utilities (power generation, grid)
- Basic Materials (copper, specialty metals, chemicals)
- Energy Equipment & Services

**Pattern to find:**
- Company beats earnings by > 10%
- Revenue growing after 2+ flat/declining quarters (THE INFLECTION)
- Stock you've never heard of (= under-followed)
- In a sector nobody talks about at dinner parties

**Quick check on hits:**
- Yahoo Finance → [TICKER] → Analysis tab → "Number of Analysts"
- < 15 analysts + earnings beat + boring sector = potential Phase 2 candidate

## Friday — "6 Months From Now" Synthesis (10 min)

Sit with coffee. One question:

> "What did I read or notice this week that the average investor will only realize in 6 months?"

Think about:
- Did Monday reveal a shortage getting worse?
- Did Wednesday surface a company you'd never considered?
- Did you hear anything in daily life about something delayed or expensive?
- Is there a connecting thread between multiple signals?

**Output:** 2-4 sentence synthesis connecting the dots.

## Monthly Roll-Up (15 min, end of month)

Re-read journal entries and ask:
1. Is one theme appearing repeatedly across all three days?
2. Has any company appeared more than once?
3. Am I confident enough to run the 7-signal framework on one name?

If yes → deep dive using `/investment-analyst`.

## Key Principle

> The market prices stories. Reality moves on supply chains. When the story lags reality, that's the opportunity.

Trade publications report supply chain reality 6 months before financial media notices. The ritual builds your lead time.
