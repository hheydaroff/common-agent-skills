# Law Overview — Structure, Scope, Definitions, Timeline

## The one mental model: 7 questions

Every provision of the AI Act answers exactly one question:

| # | Question | Answer |
|---|----------|--------|
| 1 | What is an AI system? | Art. 3 Nr. 1 |
| 2 | Who is responsible? (roles) | Art. 3, Art. 25 |
| 3 | How dangerous? (risk class) | Art. 5, 6 + Annex I/III |
| 4 | Which obligations follow? | Art. 8–27, 50, 51–55 |
| 5 | Who supervises? | Art. 64–70, 74–94 |
| 6 | What does a breach cost? | Art. 99 |
| 7 | From when does it apply? | Art. 113 |

Chain: *Was → Wer → Gefahr → Pflichten → Aufsicht → Strafe → Termin.*

## Facts & scale

- Regulation (EU) **2024/1689** · in force **01.08.2024**
- **113 articles · 13 annexes · 180 recitals**
- **Risk-based approach** + **preventive prohibition law** (präventives Verbotsgesetz)
- Seven "regulatory gaps" the Act addresses: data-drivenness, unpredictability, autonomy, opacity (black box), openness, complexity, vulnerability

## Art. 1 — Subject matter (purpose)

Improve the internal market · promote human-centric, trustworthy AI · ensure high protection of health/safety/fundamental rights (incl. democracy, rule of law, environment) · support innovation.

## Art. 2 — Scope

**Does NOT apply to:**
- AI for national security or exclusively military purposes
- AI whose sole purpose is scientific research & development
- Development before placing into service (but live/"real-world" testing IS in scope)

**Territorial reach:**
- **Marktortprinzip (market-place principle):** applies to anyone placing AI on the EU market / whose outputs are used in the EU
- **Niederlassungsprinzip (establishment principle):** deployers established in the EU

## Art. 3 — Definitions (key)

### AI system — Art. 3 Nr. 1 (5 Merkmale, OECD / recital 12)

An application is an AI system when:
1. **Maschinengestützt** — runs on computers
2. **Autonomer Betrieb** — works (to varying degrees) without human intervention
3. **Anpassungsfähigkeit** — *can* learn/adapt after deployment → **NOT mandatory**
4. **Ableitung (inference)** — derives predictions, content, recommendations, decisions from inputs
5. **Wirkung** — outputs can influence physical or virtual environments

> Only Merkmal 3 is optional. All others true → AI system.

### Other core definitions (Art. 3)
- **Anbieter (provider)** Nr. 3 · **Betreiber (deployer)** Nr. 4 · **Bevollmächtigter (authorised representative)** Nr. 5 · **Einführer (importer)** Nr. 6 · **Händler (distributor)** Nr. 7 — see `roles-and-duties.md`
- **GPAI model** Nr. 63: general usability; **> 1 billion parameters** → general usability is *presumed*
- **Systemic risk**: training > **10²⁵ FLOP** → automatic classification
- **Wesentliche Änderung (substantial modification)** Nr. 23: modification not foreseen in the original conformity assessment + affecting conformity

## Art. 4 — AI literacy (KI-Kompetenz)

- Providers AND deployers must ensure (to their best ability) that staff handling AI have sufficient AI competence
- Consider: technical knowledge, experience, education/training, deployment context, affected persons
- **Applicable since 02.02.2025** · **no fixed minimum level** (KI-Omnibus)
- 5 implementation steps: identify required competence → determine affected persons (developers vs users) → document requirements → plan & run trainings → continuous improvement

## Timeline (KI-Omnibus corrected — current)

| Date | Applies |
|------|---------|
| **01.08.2024** | In force (OJ, 20th day after publication) |

> **Base text (Art. 113):** general application 02.08.2026 · Ch. I–II from 02.02.2025 · Ch. III Sec. 4, V, VII, XII + Art. 78 from 02.08.2025 · Art. 6(1) (Annex I) from 02.08.2027.
>
> **Digital Omnibus on AI (final Aug 2026) changes:** Annex III shifted to **02.12.2027** and Annex I to **02.08.2028**; new prohibitions (deepfakes & CSAM) apply **02.12.2026**; Art. 50(2) transition **02.12.2026**; sandboxes due **02.08.2027**; machines move from Annex I Section A→B; Art. 4 "to their best extent" (no fixed minimum).
