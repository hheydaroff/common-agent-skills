# Governance, Incidents, Rights, Sanctions

## Governance — EU level (Art. 64–66)

- **AI Office (Büro für KI)** — Art. 64: established by the Commission; GPAI supervision; ~140 staff
- **European AI Board (KI-Gremium)** — Art. 65: one representative per Member State; advises/coordinates

## National (Germany)

| Body | Role |
|------|------|
| **Bundesnetzagentur (BNetzA)** | notifying authority + coordination (Art. 28) |
| **DAkkS** | accreditation of conformity-assessment bodies |
| **BSI** | notification for Annex III No. 1 (biometric) high-risk |
| Market-surveillance authority (Art. 70) | ex-post market control (Art. 74 ff.) |

Notifying authority = ex-ante (conformity assessment) · Market surveillance = ex-post.

## Conformity & certification (Art. 40–49) — key facts

- **Harmonised standards (Art. 40)** → presumption of conformity (e.g. ISO/IEC 42001, once listed)
- **Conformity assessment (Art. 43):** internal control (Annex VI) or notified body (Annex VII); **Annex VII mandatory** when standards missing/partial and for Annex III No. 1 (biometric remote ID)
- **Certificates (Art. 44):** validity max **5 years (Annex I)** / **4 years (Annex III)**, renewable
- **EU declaration of conformity (Art. 47):** keep **10 years**; **CE marking (Art. 48)**; **EU database registration (Art. 49)** before market entry

## Post-market & incidents (Art. 72–73)

- Providers must run **post-market monitoring** (Art. 72)
- **Serious incidents (Art. 73):** report to the market-surveillance authority of the MS where the incident occurred
  - If probable link: within **15 days** of knowledge
  - Otherwise: **immediately / at most 2 days**

## Rights of third parties (Art. 85–87)

- Art. 85: right to complain to the market-surveillance authority
- Art. 86: **right to explanation** — deployer of Annex III high-risk AI must explain the AI's role in an individual decision (legal/significant effects)
- Art. 87: **whistleblower protection** — Directive (EU) 2019/1937 (in DE: HinSchG)

## Sanctions (Art. 99) — "7 / 3 / 1"

| Tier | Art. 99 | Amount |
|------|---------|--------|
| **1** (prohibited practices) | Abs. 3 | **35 M€ / 7%** global annual turnover |
| **2** (provider/deployer obligations) | Abs. 4 | **15 M€ / 3%** |
| **3** (incorrect info to authorities) | Abs. 5 | **7.5 M€ / 1%** |

> Always "…**or X % of worldwide annual turnover — whichever is higher**". For large groups the percentage dominates.
> Principle: the fine scales with **societal harm**, not with procedural faults.
> **Correction:** earlier drafts of some guides say "1.5%" for tier 3 — the law says **1%**.

## Civil remedies

- Damages claims for breaches; representative actions under Directive (EU) 2020/1828 (DE: VDuG).
