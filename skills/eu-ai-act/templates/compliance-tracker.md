## Starter obligation list (EU AI Act)

- Art. 4 — AI literacy of staff
- Art. 5 — prohibited practices screening
- Art. 6 / Annex III — risk classification recorded
- Art. 9 — risk management system (high-risk providers)
- Art. 10 — data governance (training data quality, bias)
- Art. 11 — technical documentation
- Art. 12 — logging
- Art. 13 — transparency & information to deployers
- Art. 14 — human oversight
- Art. 15 — accuracy, robustness, cybersecurity
- Art. 16–26 — provider / deployer / importer / distributor duty maps
- Art. 27 — FRIA (where you are a public-body / public-service deployer)
- Art. 49 / 71 — registration in EU database
- Art. 50 — transparency for certain AI (incl. deepfakes, chatbots)
- Art. 72 / 73 — incident monitoring, reporting & information sharing

## GDPR

- Art. 35 — DPIA; Art. 36 — prior consultation
- Art. 28 — processor agreements; Art. 44–49 — transfers
- Art. 22 — automated individual decision-making
