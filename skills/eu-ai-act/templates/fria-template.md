# Fundamental Rights Impact Assessment (FRIA)

Art. 27 requires deployers of high-risk AI systems that are **public bodies** or
**private entities providing public services** to assess the system's likely
impact on fundamental rights **before** first use, and to notify the market
surveillance authority when publishing the summary. Run this even when not
strictly required — it is the recognised way to show due diligence for any
consequential decision system.
